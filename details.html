Full Description

A rare opportunity in Hamilton Park, this light-filled two-story end-unit townhome offers a spacious, flexible floor plan, a private backyard, and the easygoing lifestyle that makes this Novato neighborhood so desirable. The main level opens to a welcoming living and dining area anchored by a cozy gas fireplace, while the kitchen offers generous workspace, a breakfast bar, and an adjoining family room that can easily adapt as a casual lounge, home office, or additional dining space. A door from the family room extends the living outdoors to a generous backyard with brick paths and garden beds. A convenient half bath, laundry closet, and direct access to the attached one-car garage complete the main level.

Upstairs are two generously sized bedrooms and two full bathrooms. The primary suite features a vaulted ceiling, walk-in wardrobe, and a versatile adjoining space ideal for a home office, reading nook, fitness area, or quiet retreat. With Hamilton's parks, walking and biking trails, and open space nearby, this home brings together flexibility, convenience, and the best of Hamilton living.
